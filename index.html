Welcome to StoryEngine

StoryEngine is a platform for creating and experiencing immersive Alternate Reality Games (ARGs). Whether you're a storyteller looking to craft intricate narratives or a player eager to unravel mysteries, StoryEngine provides the tools to bring your imagination to life.

Our system simulates a convincing desktop environment where players explore stories through emails, instant messages, calendar entries, documents, images, and audio files. Stories progress through time-based release schedules and interaction-based unlocking, creating an engaging, progressive narrative experience.

✦ Key Features ✦

Rich Authoring

Create complex stories with multi-type artifacts: emails, messages, documents, calendar events, images, and audio files.

Progressive Unlocking

Release content based on time, player interactions, passwords, or conditional logic—create dynamic narratives that evolve.

Immersive Desktop

Players experience stories through a simulated desktop environment with familiar app windows and interactions.

Persistent Sessions

Stories save progress automatically, allowing players to pause and resume their journey anytime.

Zero Backend

Everything runs in the browser. No servers, no complex setup—just client-side simplicity.

Open Format

Stories are JSON-based and portable. Create once, play anywhere StoryEngine is available.
